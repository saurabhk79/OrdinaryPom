# OrdinaryPom — What it does for you

OrdinaryPom is a small Chrome extension that helps you work in focused bursts while protecting your eyes and body from long screen sessions.

## Choose your energy level

Not every work day is the same. The extension lets you pick an energy mode before you start:

- **Low Energy** — 25 minutes of focus, then a 5-minute break. Good for slower days or when you are tired.
- **Normal** — 45 minutes of focus, then a 10-minute break. Good for regular work.
- **Good** — 60 minutes of focus, then a 10-minute break. For deep, uninterrupted work.

## Gentle reminders while you focus

When you are in a focus session, OrdinaryPom reminds you to look away from the screen at two points:

- After about 20 minutes, it suggests looking away for 20 seconds.
- After about 40 minutes, it suggests giving your eyes a short rest.

These reminders are quiet and minimal. They do not stop the timer or force you to do anything.

## Calm break time

When a focus session ends, the popup changes into a break screen. It tells you to step away and suggests simple actions:

- Walk around
- Look outside
- Stretch
- Drink water

You can pause, resume, skip, or end the break at any time.

## Screen-time protection

OrdinaryPom also watches how long you have been actively using the computer. If you stay active for about 2 or 4 hours, it sends a friendly nudge to take a longer break.

If you leave the computer for 5 minutes or more, the screen-time counter resets, so it only counts real active screen time.

## See where your focus went

During focus sessions, the extension tracks only the website you are on and for how long. It does not read page contents or personal data. It then shows you your top domains so you can see where your time went.

## Daily stats

Every day, you get a simple summary:

- Total focused time
- Number of completed sessions
- Total break time
- Longest continuous focus session
- Longest continuous screen session
- Number of eye-rest reminders
- Top domains during focus

All data stays on your computer. Nothing is sent to a server.
